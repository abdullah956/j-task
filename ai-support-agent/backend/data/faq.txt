RETURN & REFUND POLICY
At ShopNest, we offer a comprehensive 30-day return policy for most items purchased through our platform. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return the item in its original condition with all tags and packaging intact for a full refund. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Please note that certain items such as personalized products, intimate apparel, and perishable goods are not eligible for return due to hygiene and safety reasons. To initiate a return, simply log into your account, navigate to your order history, and click the "Request Return" button next to the eligible item.

SHIPPING TIMES
ShopNest provides reliable shipping services both domestically and internationally to ensure your orders arrive promptly. For domestic orders within the United States, standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days, while expedited shipping options are available for delivery within 1-2 business days at an additional cost. International shipping times vary by destination country, with most orders arriving within 7-14 business days for standard international shipping. Customers can track their shipments in real-time through the tracking number provided via email once the order has been dispatched. Please be aware that international orders may be subject to customs delays and import duties which are the responsibility of the customer.

WARRANTY DETAILS
All products sold on ShopNest come with a standard manufacturer's warranty that covers defects in materials and workmanship for a period of 12 months from the date of purchase. Our premium warranty option, available for purchase at checkout, extends coverage to 24 months and includes accidental damage protection for eligible electronics and appliances. Warranty claims must be submitted through your ShopNest account with proof of purchase and a detailed description of the defect or issue. Standard warranty covers repair or replacement of defective items but does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or damage caused by accidents. Premium warranty holders receive priority processing and free shipping for warranty-related returns and replacements.

ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
ShopNest accepts a wide variety of payment methods to provide maximum convenience for our customers. We accept all major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, as well as debit cards with the Visa or Mastercard logo. Digital payment options such as PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay are also fully supported on our platform. For larger purchases over $500, we offer financing options through our partner Affirm, allowing you to split payments into manageable monthly installments. All transactions are secured with industry-standard SSL encryption and we never store your complete credit card information on our servers.

ACCOUNT & PASSWORD SECURITY POLICY
Protecting your account security is a top priority at ShopNest, and we require all customers to create strong passwords containing at least 8 characters with a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) on your account, which adds an extra layer of security by requiring a verification code sent to your mobile device during login. Never share your password with anyone, and be cautious of phishing emails claiming to be from ShopNest asking for your login credentials. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, immediately change your password and contact our security team at security@shopnest.com. ShopNest will never ask you to provide your password via email or phone.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE GOODS PROCEDURE
If you receive a damaged or defective item from ShopNest, please do not worry as we have a streamlined process to resolve the issue quickly. Contact our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos showing the damage or defect. Our team will review your claim within 24 hours and provide you with either a prepaid return label for a replacement or issue a full refund including original shipping costs. In cases of shipping damage, we will file a claim with the carrier on your behalf so you don't have to deal with the hassle. For defective items discovered after the initial 48-hour window but within 30 days, our standard return policy and warranty coverage will still apply.

SUBSCRIPTION PLAN CANCELLATION POLICY
ShopNest offers various subscription plans including ShopNest Prime for free shipping and ShopNest Subscribe & Save for recurring product deliveries, both of which can be cancelled at any time without penalty. To cancel a subscription, log into your account, go to "Manage Subscriptions" and click the "Cancel Subscription" button next to the plan you wish to terminate. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, so you will retain access to subscription benefits until that date. If you cancel within 3 days of your initial subscription sign-up, you are eligible for a full refund of the subscription fee. Any Subscribe & Save scheduled deliveries can be skipped, rescheduled, or cancelled individually without affecting your subscription status.

PROMO CODE & DISCOUNT USAGE RULES
Promotional codes and discounts at ShopNest are subject to specific terms and conditions to ensure fair usage across all customers. Each promo code can only be used once per customer account unless explicitly stated otherwise in the promotion details. Promo codes cannot be combined with other offers, sales, or discounts unless the promotion specifically allows stacking. Most promotional codes have expiration dates and minimum purchase requirements which are clearly displayed during checkout. Discounts apply only to the product price and do not reduce shipping costs, taxes, or fees unless otherwise specified. If a promo code is not working, verify that you have met all requirements and that the code has not expired, or contact customer support for assistance.

PRODUCT EXCHANGE POLICY
ShopNest makes product exchanges simple and convenient when you need a different size, color, or variant of an item you've purchased. Exchanges are available within 30 days of delivery for items in new, unused condition with all original packaging and tags attached. To request an exchange, log into your account and select "Exchange Item" from your order details, then choose your preferred replacement product. We will send you a prepaid shipping label to return the original item, and your exchange will be shipped once we receive the returned product. If there is a price difference between the original and exchange items, you will either be charged the difference or refunded accordingly. Exchange processing typically takes 7-10 business days from when we receive your returned item.
